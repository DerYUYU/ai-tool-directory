Pitch is aimed primarily at startup teams that work together on presentations. Real-time collaboration is one of the tool's strongest points — multiple team members can work on a presentation simultaneously, comment, and give feedback.

The free tier is particularly attractive: unlimited presentations without watermarks make Pitch a serious alternative for teams with a small budget. The AI features are present but not as extensive as with specialized AI presentation tools — Pitch focuses more on collaborative strength than fully automated generation.

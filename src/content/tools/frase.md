## Frase in Detail

Frase solves one of the biggest problems in SEO content marketing: the time-consuming manual research before writing. The tool automatically analyzes the top 10 results for a keyword, extracts relevant topics, questions, and key terms — and builds a structured content brief from them. Editors and freelancers save hours of prep work as a result.

The built-in AI writing assistant uses this research foundation directly when writing the article. In the editor, you can see live how well your text is optimized compared to competitors. Those who regularly produce SEO content get one of the best workflows on the market with Frase — especially compared to tools that treat AI text generation and SEO analysis separately.

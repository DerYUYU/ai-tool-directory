## Grammarly in Detail

Grammarly was founded in 2009 and now has over 30 million daily active users.
It is the most installed browser plugin for writing assistance.

### When to use Grammarly?

- Professional emails in English
- Job application letters
- Blog articles and content marketing
- Academic texts

## ProWritingAid in Detail

ProWritingAid is aimed at writers who want more than just spell-check and grammar corrections. The tool simultaneously analyzes over 20 different aspects of a text: sentence length variety, overuse of adverbs, passive constructions, repetitions, readability index, and many other parameters. The result is a detailed report that comes close to professional editorial feedback.

ProWritingAid has unique features especially for fiction and non-fiction authors: the story analysis evaluates narrative elements such as pacing and dialogue balance, while specific genre settings adjust the style to thriller, romance, or fantasy. Those who write seriously and are willing to invest time in the analysis will find here the most powerful writing feedback tool on the market.

## Pika in Detail

Pika has established itself as the most creator-friendly video AI tool. While Sora and Runway focus on quality, Pika focuses on simplicity and creativity.

### Pikaffects — the Killer Feature

Pikaffects allow you to transform existing videos or images with AI effects:

- **Squish**: Objects are compressed rubber-like
- **Explode**: Objects explode into particles
- **Melt**: Objects melt like wax
- **Inflate**: Objects puff up like balloons

These effects are perfect for viral social media posts.

### Best Use Cases

- Social media content (TikTok, Instagram Reels)
- Product visualizations
- Creative meme videos
- Quick prototypes for advertising concepts

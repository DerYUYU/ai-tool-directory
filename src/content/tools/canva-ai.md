## Canva AI in Detail

Canva was founded in 2012 and has grown into the most widely used design tool worldwide.
With AI features like Magic Design and Text to Image, it remains the king in the hobbyist segment.

### Canva AI Features

- **Magic Design**: Describe what you want, Canva generates options
- **Text to Image**: Images from text descriptions
- **Magic Eraser**: Remove objects from photos
- **Background Remover**: Remove background with one click
- **Magic Resize**: Convert design to all formats

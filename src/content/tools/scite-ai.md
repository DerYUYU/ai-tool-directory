Scite solves a problem that is often underestimated in science: Not every citation is equal. A paper can be cited because it was groundbreaking — or because a later study refuted its findings. Without Scite, this difference is not visible to the reader.

With Smart Citations, Scite automatically classifies whether a citation supports the cited study, contradicts it or mentions it neutrally. This gives researchers an immediate picture of how well a study was received in the community. This feature is unique on the market — no other tool offers this level of granularity. The lack of a free tier makes Scite less accessible, but for serious researchers the investment is justified.

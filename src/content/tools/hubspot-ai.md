HubSpot AI is not a standalone AI solution, but rather the integration of AI features into the already established HubSpot ecosystem. This means: those who already use HubSpot get AI functions directly in their familiar workflow — without a system change or data migration.

The strength lies in the breadth: HubSpot covers marketing, sales, and customer service simultaneously, and AI features are embedded in all these areas. The catch is the price — the free CRM is an excellent starting point, but as soon as teams grow and need advanced features, costs rise considerably.

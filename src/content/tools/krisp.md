Krisp installs itself as a virtual audio interface in the system and filters all background noise from the microphone and speaker signal — in real time and completely locally on the device. No cloud upload, no network latency, no privacy issue.

The tool works universally with Zoom, Teams, Discord, Google Meet, and virtually any other app, without those apps needing native integration. Remote workers and anyone communicating from noisy environments benefit immediately after installation.

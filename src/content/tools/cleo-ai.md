Cleo sets itself apart from other finance apps through its tone: instead of dry charts, the AI communicates with humor, occasional memes, and direct feedback on spending patterns. This makes budgeting more accessible to people who don't normally actively engage with their finances.

The core function is automatic transaction tracking after connecting bank accounts: Cleo categorizes spending, highlights patterns, and warns when the budget in a category is nearly exhausted. The savings features enable the automatic setting aside of small amounts — a low-threshold entry into financial discipline.

Glean solves one of the biggest productivity problems of modern teams: information is spread everywhere — in Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Jira, and dozens of other tools. Glean indexes all these sources and provides a unified, AI-powered search that not only finds documents but understands the context.

The time savings can be enormous — instead of spending five minutes searching in various tools, you find the information in seconds. This is especially noticeable in large teams where knowledge is quickly lost. The price is however not insignificant, and the initial setup requires time and technical resources.

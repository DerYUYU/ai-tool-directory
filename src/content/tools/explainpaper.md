Explainpaper solves a very specific problem: scientific papers are often barely understandable for outsiders. The tool allows you to highlight exactly the parts that are unclear — and immediately receive an understandable explanation without interrupting the reading flow.

This approach is particularly valuable for students who need to read papers from adjacent fields, or for anyone working their way into a new research area. The strength lies in the focus: Explainpaper does exactly one thing and does it well. Those who expect more — search, comparisons, systematic reviews — will need other tools.
